FAQ

Good to know.

Is Mosaic free to use?

Yes. The core widgets — To-Do, Quick Note, Daily Date and the Image Box — are free for personal use on Windows.

Does Mosaic slow down my PC?

No. Mosaic is built to stay out of your way, targeting around 18 MB of memory and under 1% CPU while idle.

Can I use my own photos instead of Pinterest?

Yes, the Image Box supports a local folder as a private, offline-friendly source in addition to a live Pinterest feed.

Will Mosaic come to macOS or mobile?

Windows ships first. macOS and mobile companion apps are on the roadmap — see the platform row below for status.
